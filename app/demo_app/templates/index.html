Vault for ICP-Brasil

A service certificate issued by the PKI engine, an ICP-Brasil certificate stored in KV, and a dynamic PostgreSQL role, each rendered into the process.

Checking rendered secrets…

Laboratory only. The ICP-Brasil card follows the DOC-ICP-04 type A1 profile. This hierarchy is not accredited by ITI, it is not chained to AC Raiz da ICP-Brasil, and the CPF in the certificate is fictitious.

The service PKI card is green while its certificate is current and turns yellow for the last 10 seconds of that certificate. A new certificate turns that card green again. The database card turns yellow for the last 10 seconds of its 2 minute credential and returns to amber when Vault renders a new role. The database password stays on the server.
